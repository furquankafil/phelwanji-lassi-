Heritage in Every Spoon

A landmark you drink from a kulhad.

The shop serves kulhad lassi — rooh afza, malai, rabdi wali — alongside garam gulab jamun, phirni, shahi tukda, moong dal halwa, rabdi falooda, kulfi falooda and kesar badam milk, exactly as listed on its current outlet listings.

Evening crowds, family party packs, late-night dessert runs after Zakir Nagar food walks — this is old-Delhi-style street-food culture with a premium taste, in earthenware.
